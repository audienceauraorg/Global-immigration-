Study in Canada

There are amazing opportunities when you study in Canada. International students are welcomed at Canadian institutions and have strong supports in place to ease with the transition and settlement process.

There are several reasons to Consider Canada for your education:

  • You can work while you study and earn money to assist with costs associated with living and studying in Canada.
  • Generally, tuition fees are much less than many other countries in the world. This, coupled with your ability to work while you study makes this an attractive reason to study in Canada.
  • There are strong supports and many international students currently studying in Canada that will assist you during your transition to Canada and your new school.
  • The quality of education in Canada is some of the best in the world. If you carry with you an education from Canada, these skills are very transferable and recognized around the world.
  • Following graduation, you could be eligible for a post graduate work permit that will allow you to remain in Canada to work for 3 full years while you work towards your permanent residency.
  • Gain valuable points towards your Express Entry or Nomination applications
  • You can bring your family with you and your spouse or common law partner can work while you study.
  • Study is a major part of the process in become a permanent resident and ultimately a Canadian Citizen.
